## Profile title

Technical SEO Audits | On-Page SEO | AEO & AI Search Readiness

## Profile overview

I help small businesses understand the technical and on-page issues affecting their websites and turn
audit findings into a practical action plan.

My workflow combines a custom SEO auditing toolkit with manual review. I check page titles, headings,
canonical tags, internal links, structured data, crawl directives and answer-oriented content signals.
Each actionable finding includes its page URL, supporting evidence and a clear next step.

You can work with me on a scoped technical audit, an SEO action plan, keyword-to-page mapping from
available research, or a review of content clarity and AI-search readiness. Where relevant, I can
analyze your Google Search Console export and compare selected competitor pages.

You receive an understandable report, a prioritized issue log and recommendations your developer or
content team can use. Before starting, I confirm your business goal, priority pages and data access.

## Project offer

**Title:** Technical SEO audit with a prioritized action plan

**Description:**
Get a clear picture of the issues found in a defined sample of your website. I combine automated
checks with manual review, identify the pages affected and explain what to fix first.

Your delivery includes a PDF report, a page-level issue log with evidence, and an implementation
roadmap. I also review relevant content and AI-search readiness signals without presenting internal
tool scores as Google rankings or measured AI visibility.

## Suggested package boundaries

| Package | Scope | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| Focused audit | One website, up to 5 fetched pages; manually verify priority findings | PDF, issue log, top-priority actions |
| Business audit | One website, up to 10 fetched pages; one supplied GSC performance export | Focused audit plus GSC observations, 30/60-day plan and mapping of up to 10 supplied keywords |
| Audit and content plan | One website, up to 15 fetched pages; up to 2 supplied competitors with 5 pages each | Business audit plus competitor comparison, up to 3 manually reviewed content briefs and 30/60/90-day roadmap |

All packages: one clarification round on delivered findings. Website editing, content publishing,
ongoing monitoring, paid data subscriptions and live backlink/AI-citation tracking are separate scope.
Keyword mapping and content briefs require manual work with the skill; the app does not automatically
conduct live keyword research. Competitor comparison uses the existing Competitive Intelligence page.
If access prevents the agreed sample, discuss scope before completing delivery.

## Client intake

- Website URL and priority services/products.
- Target country/city, language and audience.
- Main goal: qualified enquiries, sales, bookings or another measurable action.
- Priority pages and known website changes/problems.
- Optional GSC export with date range, market and device context.
- Competitor URLs and keyword research, when included in the package.
- Whether implementation is requested separately and who can approve site changes.

log. Each actionable issue includes its source page, supporting evidence and a suggested verification
step for your developer or content team.

behavior of indexability and canonical settings before making changes. I am available to clarify
the findings included in our agreed scope.

## Delivery routine

1. Agree scope and access before quoting.
2. Run the crawl and note what was actually fetched and rendered.
3. Manually check the priority findings and client-specific intent.
4. Use the project skill to draft the plan from the exported evidence.
5. Review recommendations, sources, scope and report layout.
6. Deliver the agreed files; keep website implementation a separately authorized step.
